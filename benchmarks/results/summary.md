## Protocol

5-node cluster on one machine (5 processes, loopback). raftkv-bench: closed-loop clients, 50% Put / 50% Get, 64-byte values, 10,000 keys, 10s warm-up then 60s measured per concurrency level, 3 repeats each on a fresh cluster; tables show the median of the repeats. Latency is client-observed (request sent to reply received), exact percentiles over every operation.
